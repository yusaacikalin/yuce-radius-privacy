Privacy Policy for Yuce Radius

Effective date: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Yuce Radius (“the App”), developed by Ahmet Yuşa Açıkalın, accesses, uses, stores, and protects information.

Location Data

Yuce Radius accesses precise location data, including background location, to trigger location boundary alarms created and enabled by the user. The App uses Android geofencing to detect when the user enters or exits a selected area, even when the App is closed or not in use.

Background location is used only for this core alarm functionality. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or tracking the user’s movement history.

Information Stored on the Device

Alarm information—including the alarm name, optional description, selected coordinates, location label, radius, entry or exit preference, sound, and enabled status—is stored locally on the user’s device.

Yuce Radius does not operate a developer-controlled server and does not upload alarm records or location history to such a server. Depending on the device settings, Android may include application data in system backup and restore services controlled by the user and operating-system provider.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following services to provide its functionality:

  • Google Play services: used for device location and geofencing functionality.
  • OpenStreetMap/osmdroid: used to display map tiles.
  • Android geocoding services: may process selected coordinates to display a readable address.

These services may process technical information such as the device’s IP address and network request information according to their own privacy policies.

Advertising and Analytics

The current version of Yuce Radius does not contain advertising SDKs, analytics services, or third-party crash-reporting services. Personal data is not sold or used for targeted advertising.

Permissions

The App may request precise location, background location, notifications, exact alarms, full-screen alarm access, battery optimization exemption, vibration, and related permissions required to deliver reliable location-based alarms.

Users can revoke permissions through Android system settings. Revoking location or notification permissions may prevent active alarms from functioning correctly.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored alarm data remains on the device until the user deletes the alarm, clears the App’s data, or uninstalls the App. Users can delete individual alarms directly within Yuce Radius.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to minimize data access and keep alarm information on the device. However, no device or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App’s features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
